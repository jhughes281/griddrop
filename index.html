GridDrop

Stop hunting.
The complex is already mapped.

GridDrop turns any apartment complex into a driver's map — your unit numbers pinned to the right building, the floor level called out, and the gate code on screen before you're off the main road.

1
Pick the complex

Pull from the network — complexes other drivers have already mapped.

2
Drop in your unit numbers

Type the apartments on your run. GridDrop pins each one to its building and tells you the floor.

3
Follow the pin

Gate code, turn-by-turn to the building, which stairwell, what floor. Hazards on, in and out.

One driver maps a complex once — every driver after them skips the hunt. That's the network.

Prototype with demo data. In the real app, gate codes and access notes are visible to verified couriers only, and complexes can claim & manage their listing. Try the phone: pick a complex, load a sample route, tap a stop.
